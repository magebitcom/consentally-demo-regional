How this page is set up

  1. The ConsentAlly install tag, first in <head> (store key thzbexdska6x66dg).
  2. Google's gtag.js for the Google Analytics 4 property G-5DCLB8XT3F, directly after it, written as a ConsentAlly manual tag (type="text/plain" and data-ca-src), so the browser fetches nothing from Google before ConsentAlly lets it load.

ConsentAlly sets Google Consent Mode's defaults to denied before gtag.js runs. What happens next depends on where you open the page:

  • EU and EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland: the banner asks first. Everything optional stays denied until you choose.
  • United States, every state: no banner. ConsentAlly updates Consent Mode to granted for analytics and advertising as the page loads and loads gtag.js. The ConsentAlly button in the corner ("Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" in California, "Your Privacy Choices" in other states) lets you opt out; Global Privacy Control, when your browser sends it, keeps advertising denied.
  • Anywhere else: the banner asks first, as in Europe.
